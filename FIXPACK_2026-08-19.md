### A1. Real vs bookkeeping, redefined (fix #1)
Doctrine (Edmund, verbatim intent 2026-08-18): "it doesn't matter if
it's a pure score putting something on the board or the assessor —
they are equally valid."
- A move is REAL iff it changes board membership or final tier —
  regardless of driver (score crossing, assessor stamp, override).
- "Bookkeeping" shrinks to: stamp changes with NO tier consequence.
- The assessor VETO case is also real news (CSL 2026-08-18: raw score
  crossed the entry line, assessor ruled it off the same night —
  today that renders as ledger noise "off board→None"). Render it as
  a story: crossed the line, kept off by judgment, WITH the
  assessor's stored reason when one exists — and when it doesn't,
  the story is exactly "crossed the line, kept off" with NO invented
  thesis (Edmund 2026-08-19; machinery does the assessing, Claude
  never hand-writes one). Assessor badges stay dark regardless
  (V3 #11 unchanged). Note: 'None' arrives as a STRING in
  assessed_tier — handle it explicitly, never print it to a reader.
