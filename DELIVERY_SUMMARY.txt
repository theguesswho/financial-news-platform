OPTIMIZATION STRATEGIES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Component-First (Week 3, Days 1-2)
   - Build 10 reusable UI components before pages
   - Benefit: Avoid 20+ hours of refactoring
   - Cost: 8 hours investment, saves 15+ hours

2. API-First Implementation (Week 3-5)
   - Generate TypeScript types from OpenAPI schema
   - Benefit: Perfect type safety, fewer integration bugs
   - Cost: 5 minutes setup, saves 5+ hours

3. Deploy-Once Strategy (Week 3-6)
   - Build locally Weeks 3-5, deploy Week 6
   - Benefit: Save $30-60 + 15 hours of deployment overhead
   - Cost: 2 hours final deployment, saves $30-60

4. Library-Over-Custom (Entire build)
   - Use proven libraries (React Query, Zustand, RHF)
   - Benefit: Save 20+ hours of buggy custom code
   - Cost: 3 hours learning curve, saves 20+ hours

5. Form Handling Optimization (Week 3)
   - Use React Hook Form + Zod instead of useState
   - Benefit: 70% less code, fewer bugs
   - Cost: 3 hours setup, saves 10 hours

6. Skeleton Screens & Progressive Loading (Week 5)
   - Show loading skeletons before data loads
   - Benefit: 2x faster perceived performance
   - Cost: 2 hours, saves support requests

7. Early Staging Deploy (Week 5)
   - Deploy to Vercel staging Day 5 of Week 5
   - Benefit: Find integration bugs 2 weeks before launch
   - Cost: 1 hour, saves 10 hours of Week 6 debugging
